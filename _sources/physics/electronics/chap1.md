# Electronics

## Op-Amp
Operational Amplifier

For an ideal op-amp, we assume $R_1 = \infty$, $R_0 = 0$ and $A = \infty$ where $A$ is the amplification, then the quations of the ideal op-amp are
```{math}
\begin{eqnarray*}
    I_+ ~ = ~ I_- ~ &=& ~ 0\\
    V_+ - V_- ~ &=& ~ 0\\
\end{eqnarray*}
```
